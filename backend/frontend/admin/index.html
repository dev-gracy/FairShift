📌 SDG 8 — Decent Work Signal

These metrics directly track SDG 8 Target 8.8: protecting labour rights and promoting safe working environments. High overtime and unresolved reports are leading indicators of poor worker well-being.

Explainable Risk Scores

Cohort-level scores — never individual. Each score shows exactly why it's high.

Score Formula (auditable):
Score = (long_shifts × 12) + (avg_overtime × 2) + (safety_reports × 15) + (pay_delays × 10) + (pending × 1)
Capped at 100. Low < 30 · Medium 30–59 · High ≥ 60
